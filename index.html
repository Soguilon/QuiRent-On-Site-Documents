Electricity Billing and Documentation Limitations

Electricity bills are manually handed to tenants through face-to-face interaction. Copies of the electricity bills are excluded from this documentation because they contain confidential AEC information.

Confirmed process

Face-to-face delivery of electricity bills from the business to the tenant.

Evidence limitation

Confidential bills will not be photographed, uploaded, or published on this website.

Research implication

Additional details about this procedure must be confirmed through an authorized interview or non-sensitive observation.
